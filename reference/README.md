filed here so they are in the record before any chapter names them. The
teller shows a picture only when the record names it, so nothing in here
changes what a player sees until it is written in. Placement is the
author's pen.

**Record the model and its version in `Made with` for every file filed
here.** The first four above are the author's own recollection, given on
22 September 2026; the version each was made on was not noted at the
time and the record does not guess at one. The Odonata song's row read
*not recorded* for a day: it arrived as a file with no word of what made
it, and a badge on the card was not taken for that word until the author
gave it (*MyTunes*, 2 October 2026). The version was not noted then
either. It matters for two reasons
that outlive the file. A generator's own terms are a layer over the
licence in `../CONTENT-LICENSE.md` and can bind the author whatever the
copyright position is: some services assign the output to the person
who made it, some grant only a licence back, and at least one attaches
its own non-commercial condition to free-tier output. And a picture
whose maker is unrecorded cannot be checked later against a term that
changes, or against a question about how it was made. Name the model,
its version if it has one, and the date — at the time, while it is
still a fact and not a recollection.
