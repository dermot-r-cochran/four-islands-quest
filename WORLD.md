# WORLD.md — the record and its bindings

Governance of the Kingdom of the Four Sounds, kept deliberately
minimal. This whole file is the canon apparatus; there is no other.

## The rule

**The repo is the record.** What stands on `main` is true of the
world, and there is no other authority — no hidden notes, no
ratification pipeline, no story bible. Git history is the ledger.

## The spine (binding)

The few facts that make this world this world:

- There are four islands under one Crown, and one tide-calendar;
  the High Keep stands on First Island.
- The Ferrymen's Guild crosses on any tide, and is owed for it.
- The tide-bell is not counted aloud.
- Fourth Island is not pointed at by ferrymen.
- Companies keep a seat and carry a quest.

Everything else is soft: free to be extended, revised, or
contradicted by a better idea in a later chapter.

## No hidden canon

Deliberate mysteries are unspecified in fact, not merely in text.
There is nothing behind Fourth Island until it is written; an
absence in the record is an absence in the world.

## Who writes the world

The engine (MIT) welcomes code fixes. The world content (CC BY 4.0)
is the author's pen: fork it rather than proposing story changes —
`CONTENT-LICENSE.md` describes the path, and a world you write
yourself is yours alone.

## Register

The Four Sounds stays general-audience.
